Climate8/10

The UN concedes 1.5°C will be breached — and maps the road back

Why it matters: UNEP’s first overshoot report, out today, finds no plausible pathway keeps warming below 1.5°C — current policies point to roughly 1.8°C this century, with only ~130 GtCO2 of budget left for even a coin-flip chance. But its modeling says deep cuts plus carbon removal could cap the overshoot near 0.3°C and return below the threshold by 2100.

What’s next: The report frames November’s UN climate summit and the 2035 pledge round.
