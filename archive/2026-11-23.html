The AI trade got repriced in one week — the fundamentals didn't.

Markets, boardrooms and war rooms converged on the same question this weekend: what is AI actually worth? A Fed that spent October talking about hiking — see the FOMC's December meeting — now faces a market that has already tightened for it. Ahead: a holiday-shortened week, 81.5 million Americans traveling, and very thin ice.
