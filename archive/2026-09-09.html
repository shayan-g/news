SCIENCE7/10

Comets are watering another solar system — and explaining our own

Why it matters: Lund University astronomers read flickering sodium gas in front of PDS 70 — a 5.5-million-year-old star 370 light-years away — as the signature of comets sweeping through its inner system, and their models show its two giant planets flinging icy bodies inward. SCIENCE6/10

The Americas collided millions of years earlier than the textbooks say

Why it matters: Paleomagnetic signatures in 12–6-million-year-old volcanic rocks from Colombia’s Combia province show tectonic deformation was already weak and localized when they formed — pushing the main collision between Central and South America back to the Oligocene–middle Miocene. That timing feeds straight into the Isthmus of Panama debate — and everything downstream of it, from ocean circulation to the great mammal migrations.
