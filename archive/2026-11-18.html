Science & Space

Space8/10

At 2:16 this morning, Voyager 1 became the first machine a light-day from Earth

Why it matters: Forty-nine years after launch, the probe crossed 16.1 billion miles — 25.9 billion km, about 173 astronomical units — at 10:16 UTC today, far enough that a radio command now takes a full day to arrive. Most instruments are dark and the plutonium power fades every year, but both Voyagers still phone home through the Deep Space Network.

ULA's first-ever total failure destroys 40 Amazon Leo satellites

Why it matters: Vulcan's return-to-flight Sunday — the first of 38 launches Amazon bought — failed about 10 minutes after liftoff from Cape Canaveral, the first complete mission loss in ULA's twenty-year history.
