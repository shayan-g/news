Science — lead7/10

Saturn grew a decagon: Hubble finds a 10-sided wave wrapping the south pole

Why it matters: The planet with the famous northern hexagon now has a ten-sided southern counterpart — the first large, persistent, regular-sided pattern ever seen in that hemisphere.
