The war has reached the pump

For months the US–Iran war was a shipping story; this morning it is a household one. A Houthi ballistic missile set fire to Saudi Aramco's headquarters tower, Trump is weighing joining the Saudi campaign while Tehran talks openly about attacking American warships near the Strait of Hormuz — and two hours before this briefing, September CPI printed hot at 3.7%, with gasoline alone supplying half the monthly gain. The Federal Reserve must now price a war it cannot forecast. The same infrastructure-as-target logic runs through the rest of the day: Russia put 600,000 Ukrainians in the dark days after Zelenskyy thought he had an energy ceasefire, and hackers cooked the refrigerators of French supermarkets from the internet. Oil, grids, cold chains — this is a week about energy as a weapon.
