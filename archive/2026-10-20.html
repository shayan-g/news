Astrophysics7/10

Two supermassive black holes caught on the brink of merging

Why it matters: a Max Planck Institute for Radio Astronomy team led by Silke Britzen reports in MNRAS the most compelling evidence yet of a close binary of supermassive black holes in galaxy Mrk 501, 464 million light-years away — including a 2022 alignment in which one black hole gravitationally lensed the other's jet into an Einstein ring. Planetary science7/10

A 1,800-km Martian cloud forms by physics never observed in any atmosphere

Why it matters: simulations led by Jorge Hernández-Bernal can only reproduce the Arsia Mons Elongated Cloud if water vapor freezes directly into ice with no dust seeds — homogeneous nucleation, a textbook process never before observed in a planetary atmosphere, demanding supersaturation roughly 100,000× anything in Earth's skies.

What's next: the authors suggest the mechanism may also operate in high, cold clouds on Earth and Venus — unconfirmed for now.
