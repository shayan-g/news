The bill for a war economy comes due at 2pm Eastern.

The Federal Reserve is expected to raise interest rates today for the first time since 2023 — markets put the odds at 91% — because the war with Iran has done what no forecast quite priced in: pushed the 10-year Treasury to its first close above 5% since 2007 and Brent crude near $109 with Saudi Arabia's Hormuz-bypass pipeline shut by drone strikes. The politics are moving with the prices — the House voted a third time to end the war, with record Republican defections, and UK inflation just jumped back above 3% on pump prices alone. Against all of that, the AI economy refuses to decelerate: Anthropic told investors profit has arrived ahead of a Nasdaq IPO that could value it at $2 trillion, ByteDance borrowed $29.6 billion, and China's Shanghai AI Lab gave away a frontier-scale agent for free. Two economies are running at different speeds, and today Kevin Warsh has to set one price for both.
