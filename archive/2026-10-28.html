The AI build-out is being financed at conviction. Everything around it is being priced at doubt.

Anthropic filed to go public on Monday and lined up Broadcom to lease its next generation of chips, two commitments that together price the AI build-out somewhere north of anything this cycle has attempted. The same week, Microsoft offered buyouts to 60,000 people - a quarter of its staff - which is what the other side of that trade looks like when it reaches payroll. Into that, Kevin Warsh is expected to hold at 3.75-4.00% with inflation at 3.7% and a December hike most of the market has already accepted. The two halves meet this afternoon: Microsoft and Alphabet report hours after Warsh explains why he is standing still.

The brain may flicker between what you see and what it hides, 15 times a second

Why it matters: Recording face-processing neurons in two macaques with Neuropixels probes, Janis Hesse and Doris Tsao report that during a single steady percept the brain alternates about 15 times per second between patterns encoding the seen and the suppressed image - faces reconstructable from each micro-epoch.

What's next: The work is a bioRxiv preprint from October 16 and is not yet peer reviewed; it rests on two animals, which outside researchers flag as the central caveat.
