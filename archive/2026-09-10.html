SCIENCE8/10

Three Denisovans, 1,300 tools, 120,000 years: a cave in Yunnan opens our cousins’ longest record

Why it matters: Two Nature papers describe Bianfu Cave in Yunnan: four teeth, an arm-bone fragment and two skull pieces from at least three Denisovans who lived roughly 167,000–134,000 years ago, beside butchered water buffalo and gaur and more than 1,300 stone and bone tools. Five hours to make the Moon: rock strength rewrites the giant-impact story

Why it matters: SwRI-led simulations re-ran the Theia impact with temperature-dependent material strength — instead of treating both bodies as strengthless fluid — and found cooler, stronger bodies can fling off an intact Moon within about five hours, no slow debris-disk assembly required.
