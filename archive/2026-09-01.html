The ingredients for rocky planets existed 100 million years after the Big Bang

Why it matters: University of Portsmouth simulations show the first stars’ pair-instability supernovae — single blasts ejecting over 100 solar masses of carbon, oxygen and iron — could seed terrestrial-planet formation around long-lived low-mass stars before the first galaxies existed, billions of years earlier than assumed.

Spaceflight6/10

NASA satellites navigated space using debris as landmarks — no GPS

Why it matters: The FALCON experiment on NASA’s four-satellite Starling swarm used onboard cameras and a catalog of known objects to fix its own position with no GPS signal — and autonomously refined the orbits of more than 200 tracked objects in three days. It’s the navigation layer lunar and deep-space missions will need where GPS doesn’t reach.
