History's biggest IPO listed into a market that stopped believing.

Anthropic banked $119.5 billion and closed its first day up 17% — and the tape treated even that as a selling opportunity. World9/10

The World Court finds Myanmar committed genocide — a first against any state

Why it matters: The ICJ ruled 9–6 for The Gambia on Thursday that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in Rakhine State, with court president Yuji Iwasawa citing some 10,000 killed in the 2017 crackdown. In eight decades, no state had ever been held responsible for genocide by the court — a precedent that will be read closely in South Africa's Gaza case.

What's next: Myanmar is ordered to prosecute perpetrators and pay compensation set in a later phase — though the court has no enforcement arm.

What's next: NASA aims to restore operations in the last week of November; one test already got the stuck wheel moving.
