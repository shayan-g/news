Claude helped find four new vault particles — 40 years after the originals stumped biology

Why it matters: Vault particles — giant protein shells in nearly every eukaryotic cell, function unknown since 1986 — just got relatives: Claude searched ~15 billion predicted proteins, and of 44 lab-tested candidates, seven assembled and four were confirmed by cryo-EM.
