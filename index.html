The board blinked, and the whole market moved

Nvidia's directors spent ten days insisting Jensen Huang would not return; on Monday they reinstated him anyway — with Trump claiming credit — and a single governance reversal produced the Nasdaq's best day since April 2025, a 31% Nvidia moonshot, and half of last week's correction clawed back in one session. Washington's response was not relief but fury: Huang now faces four congressional panels in the first week of December over chips that may have reached China. The war economy, meanwhile, runs in the opposite direction — tankers are hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz under US Navy escort, Ukraine is sinking Russian warships as far afield as Benghazi, and Brent still closed at its lowest since August. Add nearly $85 billion of M&A in roughly a day — CrowdStrike–Zscaler, Sanofi–Insmed — and you get a market that has decided the correction is over, even as the Fed debates whether December brings a 25 or a 50 basis-point hike.
