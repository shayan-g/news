The AI trade just lost the man who built it.

It is a day about single points of failure — in companies, in chokepoints, and in the price of money.

Tech & AI9/10

Jensen Huang resigns from Nvidia, effective immediately

Why it matters: The man who spent 33 years building Nvidia into the anchor of the AI economy quit as president, CEO and board member on Nov 12, after admitting infidelity amid reports tying one relationship to a suspected Chinese intelligence asset.
