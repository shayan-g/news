The war premium is coming out of everything.

Eight months of war closed the Strait of Hormuz and put a fear tax on every asset — and on Monday the market decided the tax is ending: Brent fell 5.85%, the S&P 500 had its best day since April, and the VIX closed under 20 for the first time since the crash. The catalyst was less a deal than a tone, with Washington and Tehran still talking through Qatar and Trump signaling no new strikes. Into that window steps Anthropic, which launched a roadshow this morning for a Nasdaq listing that would out-raise every IPO in history except SpaceX’s five months ago — even as Nvidia, the last cycle’s standard-bearer, fell against the rally while its board investigates Jensen Huang. A week after the midterms handed Democrats both chambers, the macro question has flipped from how bad it gets to how fast risk unwinds — and October CPI lands this morning to test the answer.
