Science7/10

Deep magma can hoard oxygen, which may be how Earth’s air got its supply

Why it matters: Melting synthetic basalt at pressures up to 7 gigapascals, an HPSTAR-led team found melts holding up to 5.2% excess oxygen by weight in peroxide-like bonds — essentially zero at surface pressure — suggesting rising magma releases oxidizing power as pressure drops. The timing lines up with the Great Oxidation Event window 2.5–2.0 billion years ago, when large igneous provinces were erupting.

What’s next: The authors are explicit that this is a mechanism, not a verdict: natural relevance depends on how oxidized the source mantle regions actually were.
